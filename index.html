Apeiron

Apeiron starts with you, then pulls back one scale at a time. Each step is a change of distance, not just a change of view.

You
Your surroundings
Earth
The Moon
The Sun
The Solar System
The Milky Way
The Local Neighbourhood
The Observable Universe

Some things that feel enormous up close get quieter when you see how much space they actually take up.

Apeiron doesn't try to make anything feel small on purpose. It just shows the scale, and lets that do the work.

Currently in development, with no release date yet.

BakeCost

BakeCost is a simple costing and pricing calculator built for home bakers and small kitchens. Enter your ingredients, quantities and overheads, and it works out what to charge — no spreadsheet required.

It's built to be quick to open, quick to use, and to stay out of your way otherwise.

BakeCost — Privacy Policy

Last updated 26 September 2026

BakeCost is built to work entirely on your device. It does not require an account, and it does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

Recipes, ingredients, quantities and pricing you enter are saved locally on your device only. Northloom cannot see this data, and it is never sent anywhere.

If this ever changes — for example, to add an optional backup feature — this page will be updated first, and the change will also be reflected in BakeCost's Play Store listing.

Questions about this policy can be sent to northloom.apps@gmail.com.
